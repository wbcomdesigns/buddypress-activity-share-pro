# Frequently asked questions

**Does Activity Share Pro work without the free plugin?**
No. Activity Share Pro is an add-on. Install and activate Activity Share for BuddyPress first. WordPress refuses to activate Pro while the free plugin is missing or off, and tells you to activate it first. If you deactivate the free plugin later, Pro pauses with one notice and a button to activate it again. Your Pro settings are kept.

**Do I need a license key to use Activity Share Pro?**
No. Every Pro feature works with or without an active license key. The license only controls automatic updates.

**Can visitors repost or send a post to a friend?**
No. Visitors see the **Repost** and **Send to a friend** rows, but choosing one opens a **Join the conversation** box asking them to log in or register.

**Can members repost their own posts?**
Only into a group they belong to. Members cannot repost their own post onto their own profile.

**Can a member stop others reposting their posts?**
Yes. Under **Settings > General** on their profile, they can untick **Allow other members to repost my posts**.

**Can I stop members sharing posts from a group?**
Group admins of a public group can untick the sharing choices in the **Sharing** box under **Manage > Settings**. Posts in private and hidden groups never share outside and can only be reposted inside the group.

**What happens when someone reposts a post and the original is deleted?**
The repost stays, and its quoted card reads "This post is no longer available." or "This reply is no longer available."

**Can members undo a repost?**
Yes. After a quick repost an **Undo** message appears, and **Undo repost** stays in the Share menu on the post.

**Do share counts include clicks on Facebook or X?**
No. The number next to the Share button counts reposts only. Clicks on network buttons appear in the **Analytics** tab when **Share analytics** is on.

**Do you store visitor IP addresses?**
No. With Share analytics on, visitors are counted with a one-way code that changes every day.

**How do I give members points for shares?**
Install and activate WB Gamification, GamiPress or myCRED. Pro adds two rewards to it: one when a shared post brings a visitor and one when it brings a new member. Set the points in that plugin and keep **Short share links** on.

**Which content types can members share?**
Any public post type you tick on the **Content types** tab, such as posts, pages, products or courses.

**Can I change the notification email?**
Yes. The repost email is a BuddyPress email called "A member reposts one of your updates." under **Appearance > Emails**.
