# Supported Social Networks

This page lists every external sharing destination the plugin supports. Your administrator controls which ones appear in the Share menu and in what order.

## External sharing networks

### Facebook

Opens the Facebook share dialog. Facebook reads the activity page's Open Graph tags to build a preview.

### X (Twitter)

Opens a pre-filled post on X with the activity title and link. X has its own character limit; long titles may be trimmed by the platform.

### LinkedIn

Opens the LinkedIn share form with the activity link pre-filled.

### WhatsApp

Opens WhatsApp with the activity link ready to send. On mobile it launches the app; on desktop it opens WhatsApp Web.

### Telegram

Opens Telegram's share interface with the activity link and title pre-filled. On mobile it may open the Telegram app.

### Pinterest

Opens the Pinterest Create Pin page with the activity link and description. Pinterest works best when the activity contains an image.

### Reddit

Opens the Reddit submit-a-link form with the title and link pre-filled. You choose the subreddit after the form opens.

### WordPress

Opens the WordPress.com Press This form with the activity link, for quickly republishing to a WordPress.com blog.

### Pocket

Saves the activity link to your Pocket reading list. You need to be logged in to Pocket in that browser session.

### Bluesky

Opens a Bluesky compose window with a pre-filled message that includes the activity title and link.

### E-mail

Opens your operating system's default email client with a pre-filled subject and body. The subject includes your site name and the activity title; the body includes the activity link. You add recipients and send from your own client. The plugin does not send email on your behalf.

### Copy Link

Copies the activity's permalink to your clipboard. A small "Link Copied!" tooltip confirms the action. No new tab or window opens.

## Internal reshare destinations

These are not social networks; they are destinations inside your community. See [Resharing to Your Profile, a Group, or a Friend](./02-resharing-internally.md) for details.

| Destination | Requires |
|---|---|
| My Profile | Being logged in |
| A Group | Being a member of that group and the Groups component being active |
| A Friend | The Friends component being active and having confirmed friends |

## Which networks does my site show?

The Share menu shows only the networks your administrator has enabled, in the order they set. If a network you want is missing, contact your site administrator.
